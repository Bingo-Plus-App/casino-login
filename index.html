Account Security & Phishing Awareness

Online gaming accounts can be targets for imitation pages and fraudulent messages.

Watch for lookalike domains

Carefully inspect the full address bar. A familiar logo or a padlock icon does not establish that a website is operated by the company it appears to represent.

Never disclose an OTP

One-time passwords, account recovery codes, and payment credentials should never be shared through chat messages or unsolicited calls.

Be cautious with downloads

Do not install unknown APK files or browser extensions that promise account access, free credit, or guaranteed gaming results.

Check eligibility and local rules

Real-money gaming is restricted to eligible adults aged 21+ where permitted by applicable law. Review terms, licensing claims, and responsible gaming resources before participating.
